CSV Import Format
Accepts standard CSV with or without a header row. Pool elevation, full pool, and delta are fetched automatically from the USGS API. Supported column names:
action, rating, logged_at, notes
  • action: in (launch) or out (haul)
  • rating: green, yellow, or red
  • logged_at: ISO/RFC3339 (e.g. 2024-06-01T14:00:00Z) or YYYY-MM-DD
  • notes: optional comments / observations
